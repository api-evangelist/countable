## Product

Actionable is a self-service advocacy platform for launching, managing and measuring
campaigns without developers or consultants. Documented campaign types: contacting
elected officials, petitions and signature gathering, pledges, supporter video story
collection, lead capture, and polling. It provides real-time in-platform reporting on
conversion rate, participation, supporter growth and engagement across campaigns.
Self-service pricing published at the time of this pass: $29.99/month or $299.99/year
for three campaigns, with higher tiers up to enterprise available on request.
